COMPILED INSTRUCTION
==============================================================================

Analyze the legal provision and extract all mandate components in a single pass.

**Step 1: Determine is_mandate**
Answer TRUE only if the provision obligates a federal officer, agency, commission, or entity to deliver a report, study, notification, certification, plan, or other information TO Congress (including chambers, committees, subcommittees, leadership, or the Comptroller General acting for Congress).

Answer FALSE if:
- The recipient is not Congress (agency head, court, state, public, registry)
- The text only defines terms, authorizes appropriations, grants rulemaking authority, or establishes a body without requiring congressional reporting
- It merely cross-references a reporting duty created elsewhere

**Step 2: Extract recipient**
Identify who receives the mandate: "congress", "congressional_committee", specific agency names, or "not_congress". Be specific when the text names particular committees or chambers.

**Step 3: Extract reporting_entity**
Identify who must fulfill the mandate: the Task Force, Administrator, Secretary, agency, commission, or other entity obligated to report or notify. If unclear from the text, state "unclear" or "not specified."

**Step 4: Extract deadline**
Quote the exact temporal language from the text: "not later than 90 days after December 23, 2024", "promptly", "within 180 days after notice", "March 31, 2025." If no deadline exists, state "not specified."

**Step 5: Determine frequency** (CRITICAL—most error-prone field)
- **"one-time"**: Single discharge with no repetition signal. "Not later than 180 days after enactment" with no "thereafter" or "annually" = one-time.
- **"annual" / "biennial" / "quarterly" / "monthly"**: Requires explicit repetition language: "annually", "each year", "every 2 years", "for each fiscal year", "and annually thereafter", "quarterly basis." Look for "thereafter", "each", "every."
- **"event-driven"**: Recurs conditionally: "upon each determination", "whenever the Secretary finds", "in the case of any such waiver."
- **"unknown"**: Duty stated but timing unclear.

Remember: A deadline is NOT a cadence. Decide from the text, not the topic.

**Step 6: Assess confidence**
- **"high"**: Clear mandate language, explicit recipient, identifiable reporting entity, and unambiguous deadline/frequency.
- **"medium"**: Some ambiguity in one or two components but core mandate is evident.
- **"low"**: Substantial uncertainty in interpretation, vague language, or missing critical elements.

Output all six fields: is_mandate, recipient, reporting_entity, deadline, frequency, confidence.
